Games turn repetition into something children want to come back to. On Jacobmatematik, times tables practice comes as short, playful rounds with characters like Paul Practice and Isaac Isuck – free, in the browser, with no download.

Why games help with times tables

Memorising 7 × 8 is dull on a worksheet. A two-minute timed round with a score to beat makes the same fact stick. Short sessions also fit homework and classroom rotation: five minutes of drill, then on to something else.

What you can play

Times-tables drill fills the 1–9 multiplication table against the clock. Beat your own best time. Afterwards you see which facts were slow – that is your next practice list.

Small table is calmer: one fact at a time until it sticks. Use it before you chase speed.

Isaac Isuck FPS is a maths game with more action for pupils who need a bigger reward loop after solid drill work.

Tips for parents and teachers

  1. Start with accuracy, then add speed.
  2. Celebrate personal bests, not class rankings.
  3. Mix a calm round and a timed round in the same sitting.
  4. Guest mode is fine for a first try; accounts save progress for school use.
