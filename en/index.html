Jacobmatematik is free online maths practice for primary school. Build a strong foundation with short drills, Maths Tower and Isaac Isuck – maths that feels like a game. Start from this page and the main exercises open in English. You can be practising in under 10 minutes.

What you can practise

  • Times tables – learn the 1–10 multiplication table with instant feedback
  • Times tables games – timed drills and playful rounds with characters
  • Fractions – simplify, add, subtract and multiply step by step
  • Order of operations – brackets first, then multiply and divide before add and subtract

For students

Short sessions beat long Sundays. Practise a little every day, see your score improve, and unlock games when the skills stick.

For teachers

Set a class going without heavy setup. Pupils can start as guests; with school accounts you get an overview of who needs help.

Frequently asked questions

Is Jacobmatematik free?

Yes. All practice on Jacobmatematik is free, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest straight away. Create an account if you want to save results, and schools can follow a whole class.

What language are the exercises in?

Start from this page and the main exercises open in English. Your choice stays after login and reload. Telling the time, the pencil-case game, Marley’s shop and Isaac Isuck’s spoken lines are still in Danish. This is not a finished translation of every game.
