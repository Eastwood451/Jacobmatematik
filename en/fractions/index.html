Fractions show parts of a whole. Once pupils can simplify and compare fractions, addition, subtraction and multiplication become much easier. Jacobmatematik walks through each skill with immediate feedback – free and in the browser.

What a fraction is

In the fraction 3/4, the bottom number (denominator) says how many equal parts the whole is split into. The top number (numerator) says how many of those parts you have. Pizza slices and chocolate bars are the classic picture – and they work because the parts are equal.

Skills you can practise

  • Simplify – reduce a fraction to lowest terms by dividing by a common factor.
  • Add and subtract – find a common denominator, then work with the numerators.
  • Multiply – multiply numerators together and denominators together, then simplify.

A simple order that works

  1. Understand equal parts (pictures before symbols).
  2. Simplify until it feels automatic.
  3. Add and subtract with the same denominator, then different denominators.
  4. Multiply, and check by estimating whether the answer should be larger or smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Is fraction practice free?

Yes. All exercises on Jacobmatematik are free and ad-free.

Do I need worksheets?

No. Practice runs online with instant feedback. You can still use paper alongside if you like.

What should my child learn first?

Equal parts and simplifying. Then add and subtract with the same denominator before moving on.
