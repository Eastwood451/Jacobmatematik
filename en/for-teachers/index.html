Maths practice for your class – with an overview

Log in as a teacherSee pupil games

Set meaningful practice without heavy setup. Pupils train times tables, fractions and number sense; with school accounts you can see who is stuck and who is flying.

What teachers get

  • Free practice pupils can start the same day.
  • Guest mode for a quick try; accounts when you need saved results.
  • Class overview in the teacher portal so support goes to the right pupils.
  • Topic pages you can share (times tables, fractions, order of operations).

Suggested classroom use

  1. Two minutes of calm times-tables practice as a starter.
  2. A timed drill for those who are ready.
  3. Fraction or order-of-operations rounds in stations.
  4. Check the overview after the lesson and plan tomorrow’s support.

The product UI is currently in Danish. Number drills still work for English-speaking classes; English landing pages help you brief pupils and parents.
